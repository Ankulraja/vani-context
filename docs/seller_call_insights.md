# What the seller dataset says about calls (analysed 10 Oct 2026)

Source: organiser seller dataset (5,000 sellers, 1 Apr–2 Oct 2026). Counts are keyword matches on
`lead_call_summary`, VANI transcripts and WhatsApp chatbot turns; treat percentages as directional.

## A. IndiaMART → seller (VANI bot calls: 9,657 answered calls, 4,395 sellers)

**What VANI calls about** (share of call summaries)
| Pitch | Share |
|---|---|
| Offers a meeting with a sales executive | 30 % |
| Paid services / membership | 12 % |
| "You were checking buy-leads" | 12 % |
| "Buyers in your area are enquiring" | 12 % |
| Improve your profile / catalog | 10 % |
| Follow-up on a callback the seller asked for | 4.5 % |

**How the calls end**
| Outcome | Share |
|---|---|
| Not Interested (disposition) | 42 % |
| General (talked, nothing agreed) | 30 % |
| Call later / busy | 16 % |
| Meeting fixed | 11 % |

Underneath the dispositions: **19 % of calls are cut abruptly by the seller**, 14 % end with "busy, call later",
2.2 % reach a wrong person, 1.4 % ask for WhatsApp/email instead, 1.4 % say they already have a paid plan or
already met the executive, 1 % complain that paid leads gave no results, 0.8 % deny ever asking for a callback.
Average answered call: 36 seconds.

**Most common seller lines on VANI calls** (2,287 transcript turns): busy / call later (11 %), "ok send the
executive" (4 %), "who is this / why are you calling / is this a bot" (3 %), not interested (2.4 %), free
listing is enough (2 %), already paid / already talked to your executive (2 %), what does the plan cost (1.7 %).

**Repeat-call problem (the memory gap):** sellers are called 3.8 times on average; 1,419 sellers 5+ times and
341 sellers 10+ times. 694 sellers said Not Interested at least twice, and 496 of them were still called 5 or
more times. 65 % of attempts are answered. Recorded objections on profiles: unavailability / scheduling,
call-quality failures, no response, explicit refusal, wrong contact, "already handled by an executive".

**521 of the 1,267 sellers with enquiries in 90 days replied to none of them** — the most useful thing an
IndiaMART call can do for a seller is to read out and help answer those enquiries, not pitch a meeting.

## B. Seller → IndiaMART (WhatsApp chatbot: 47,507 seller-typed turns, 2,794 sellers)

**89 % of seller messages to IndiaMART's WhatsApp are the buy-lead flow**: the seller is acting as a BUYER
(posting a requirement, answering quantity / spec / city prompts, "need best price for …"). The top typed
words are quantities and specs: "100", "50", "plastic", "stainless steel", "for reselling".

The remaining 11 % (3,717 turns, 896 sellers) are the real support asks, by intent:
| Ask | Turns |
|---|---|
| Photo / document uploaded but not added as a product (catalog help) | 925 |
| Product price / product details / product name questions | 215 |
| Phone number / supplier contact number | 172 |
| Catalog management | 91 |
| "Need more sellers" (as a buyer) | 72 |
| Stop calls from IndiaMART | 23 |
| Customer care contact / raise a complaint / call me | ~40 |
| "I want more details about paid services, arrange a callback" | 11 |

Executive calls (2,580, Aug–Sep): 57 % answered, average 2.3 minutes of talk; almost all are DSR follow-ups.

## What this means for Tanya (VANI) and the memory file

1. **Stop the 23rd pitch**: if the memory shows Not Interested twice or "do not call", the opener must not
   offer a meeting or paid services again; ask only about enquiries or say nothing new.
2. **Open with the seller's enquiries, not IndiaMART's pitch**: unread enquiries are the one topic sellers
   care about; 41 % of sellers with enquiries have answered none.
3. **Respect "busy / call later"**: record the asked time and open the next call with it ("aapne 11 baje
   bola tha").
4. **Remember "already met the executive / already paid"**: 1.4 % of calls and 3 % of profiles; the memory
   file flags it and the prompt must not re-pitch.
5. **Handle "kaun bol raha hai / bot ho?"**: 3 % of seller turns; a one-line honest answer and continue.
6. **Offer WhatsApp for details**: sellers ask for it, and the catalog / photo-upload asks on WhatsApp show
   that sellers already use that channel for IndiaMART tasks.
7. **Seller on WhatsApp is often buying**: when a seller writes a requirement, treat them as a buyer in that
   thread (quantity, city, best price), not as a seller.
